# Writing rules (consulting-grade brochure copy)

1. **Action titles.** The title states the conclusion, not the topic. "Everyone can define Scope 3. *Few can defend it.*" not "Why this programme". Two lines maximum; the italic second line turns or sharpens the first.
2. **One message per page.** If a page needs two headlines, it is two pages.
3. **Pyramid order.** Title (the answer), subtitle (the reason), blocks (the evidence), caption (the caveat).
4. **Numbers carry units and sources.** "10,500+ participants trained" comes from `{{fact.participants_trained}}`. Market statistics need a named source in the block's `source` field; if there is none, leave the chart out.
5. **Concrete over abstract.** Name the artefact ("a Scope 1 and 2 inventory for a real-shaped manufacturing entity"), the standard ("SBTi Corporate Net-Zero Standard"), the reviewer ("a practitioner").
6. **Short parallel bullets.** 3 per module, each under 14 words, starting with a noun or verb consistently.
7. **British English, ESGPro house style:** programme, decarbonisation, organisation; en dash for ranges (10–15 hours); ₹ with Indian digit grouping (₹74,990).
8. **Honest boundaries.** Keep the disclaimers: institute-reported figures; outcomes not guaranteed; names used with permission; what the fee excludes; the credential is not a statutory licence.
9. **No filler.** Cut "world-class", "cutting-edge", "unlock your potential", "in today's fast-paced world".
10. **Consistency across programmes.** Sibling programmes share the mentor, facts, alumni and page architecture. Their differences (fee, week-4 content, duration) must be explicit on the cover and pricing page.
